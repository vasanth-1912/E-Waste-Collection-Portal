About Us

About EcoCycle

Zero Landfill Target Safe Circular Economy

Transforming Electronic Waste Into Sustainable Resources

Electronic waste (e-waste) is one of the fastest-growing waste streams in the world. When discarded improperly in regular trash, toxic substances such as lead, mercury, and cadmium contaminate our soil and water resources.

EcoCycle provides an effortless, transparent bridge between responsible citizens, institutions, and government-authorized e-waste recycling centers. We ensure every retired device is either refurbished, stripped for reusable components, or safely dismantled with zero ecological harm.

Reduce Pollution

Prevents toxic heavy metals and chemicals from polluting our ground water and ecosystems.

Reuse Resources

Recovers valuable raw materials including gold, copper, and rare earths for circular production.

Protect Nature

Conserves raw natural minerals, drastically cutting down harmful mining and carbon emissions.
